# Portfolio Website Checklist — Tech + Creative Freelancer

A content inventory for a hybrid **technology + creative** freelancer
(e.g. a Shopify Developer who also does video editing and graphic design).

> **The core challenge:** you're selling two things to two audiences — a store
> owner who needs a Shopify dev, and a brand who needs video/graphics. The site
> must serve both without feeling scattered.
>
> **Your differentiator:** you can deliver a *whole* project — the store, the
> videos, and the graphics — under one roof. Most freelancers do one. Make the
> "one person, full package" angle the spine of the site.

*(Design/vibes come later — this is about what content to gather and include.)*

---

## 1. Homepage / Hero
- [ ] One-line positioning statement (who you are + what you do + who you help)
- [ ] Sub-headline clarifying the dual offer (tech + creative)
- [ ] Primary call-to-action (Book a call / See my work / Get a quote)
- [ ] Hero visual or short showreel (the homepage itself is a portfolio piece)
- [ ] Trust strip — client logos, platform badges (Shopify Partner, Adobe), or key stats

## 2. Work / Portfolio (the core)
Organize by **type** (Development / Video / Design) or by **project** (showing
you did all three on one job — your unique selling point).

For each project entry:
- [ ] Thumbnail + title
- [ ] Client / brand name (or "confidential client" if under NDA)
- [ ] Category tags (Shopify, Liquid, video edit, motion graphics, brand design…)
- [ ] The problem they came with
- [ ] What you did (your specific role / deliverables)
- [ ] The outcome — metrics if possible (conversion lift, load speed, views, engagement)
- [ ] Media (live site link, before/after, embedded video, image gallery)
- [ ] Tools used

Content to gather:
- [ ] 3–6 strong case studies (depth over quantity)
- [ ] A video reel (60–90 sec) of your best edits / motion work
- [ ] A design gallery (thumbnails, social graphics, product photography, mockups)
- [ ] Live Shopify store links or screenshots (demo/staging store if client sites are private)
- [ ] Code samples / theme customizations you can show

## 3. Services
Package offers into buckets since you span disciplines:
- [ ] **Shopify Development** — theme dev, custom Liquid, app integration, migrations, speed optimization, headless
- [ ] **Video** — editing, short-form (Reels/TikTok), product videos, motion graphics
- [ ] **Graphic Design** — brand assets, social templates, product imagery, ad creative
- [ ] **The combo pitch** — "full store + content" packages only you can offer
- [ ] For each: short description, ideal-for, optional starting price ("from $X")

## 4. About
- [ ] Photo of you (people hire people)
- [ ] Your story — the tech + creative combo and why it's an advantage
- [ ] Your process / how you work with clients
- [ ] Personality (this section can carry the "vibe")

## 5. Skills / Tools
- [ ] Tech stack — Shopify, Liquid, HTML/CSS/JS, theme frameworks, apps, APIs
- [ ] Creative stack — Premiere / After Effects / DaVinci, Photoshop / Illustrator / Figma
- [ ] Keep it visual (icons/logos) rather than a wall of text

## 6. Social Proof
- [ ] Testimonials (name, role, company, photo — specificity = credibility)
- [ ] Client logos (with permission)
- [ ] Results / metrics pulled out as highlights
- [ ] Links to live stores, YouTube/Vimeo, Instagram/Behance/Dribbble

## 7. Contact / Conversion
- [ ] Contact form (name, email, project type, budget, message)
- [ ] Direct email and/or booking link (Calendly)
- [ ] Social links — especially those showing creative work
- [ ] Optional: rates / availability status ("Booking for Q4")

## 8. Supporting / Trust Pages
- [ ] Downloadable resume or one-pager
- [ ] FAQ (process, timelines, pricing, revisions, ownership)
- [ ] Blog / process notes (optional — good for SEO + expertise)
- [ ] Footer (quick nav, socials, copyright, back-to-top)

---

## Assets to Collect First (the real bottleneck)
Gather these before touching design:

- [ ] Professional headshot
- [ ] 3–6 case studies written up (problem → work → result)
- [ ] Metrics/results for each project (dig up analytics, ask past clients)
- [ ] Video showreel cut and exported
- [ ] Design work collected and cleaned up (mockups make raw graphics look pro)
- [ ] Live/demo store links or high-res screenshots
- [ ] 3–5 testimonials (ask past clients now — this takes the longest)
- [ ] Client logos (with permission)
- [ ] Bio written in your voice
- [ ] Services + rough pricing decided
- [ ] Tool/skill list finalized
- [ ] Contact method + booking link set up

---

## Strategic Note
Lead with the "one person, full package" angle throughout. Presenting yourself
as two separate freelancers stapled together dilutes the pitch; presenting
yourself as the rare person who can build the store *and* create the content
that sells through it is what wins the higher-value work.
