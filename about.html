我 My story

How I got here

I started freelancing in 2024 while studying Computer Science, editing videos for a medical content agency. That led to Earth Moon Magick, a spiritual and wellness brand, where I now work as a Shopify Technical Specialist and video editor: building pages in GemPages, making theme edits in Liquid, HTML and CSS, and cutting the Meta ads that bring people to the store.

At school I’ve built Android apps and worked on FSLAMES (Filipino Sign Language Automated Mobile Education System), a learning app with machine-learning sign recognition. Alongside that I’ve been earning certifications in IT, data science and cybersecurity. Next, I want to grow as a front-end developer and go deeper into security.
