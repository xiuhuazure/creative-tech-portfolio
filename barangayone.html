BarangayONE: barangay documents, requested from home.

A final requirement group project: a prototype that lets residents of Brgy. Sinauna request barangay documents, track their status, and find announcements and emergency hotlines in one mobile app, while barangay staff review, approve and release every request from a web dashboard.

The problem

A trip to the hall, then another

Getting a barangay clearance often means a trip to the hall, a wait in line, and a second trip just to ask whether it is ready. Residents cannot see where their request is, and staff answer the same questions every day.

What we built

Two parts, one set of records

1. Resident mobile app

Residents sign up with their mobile number and a one-time code, choose a Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency or Certificate of Indigency, see the requirements first, attach their ID and submit. Each request gets a reference number and a five-step timeline: Submitted, Under Review, For Approval, Ready for Pickup and Completed. If staff return it, the resident sees the note and can resubmit. Emergency hotlines, announcements and barangay info are open to everyone without an account.

2. Staff web dashboard

Authorized personnel see every request in one queue, filter by status, and start a review, return a request with a note, reject it with a reason, approve it, or mark it as released. They also publish announcements, keep hotlines and office details current, view monthly reports, manage Clerk, Official and Admin accounts, and check an audit log of every change.

My role

The HTML coder

I was the team’s HTML coder (front-end developer). I turned the designs into working pages:

  • Built the standalone clickable prototype in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the resident app and the staff dashboard sharing one set of data.
  • Built the public landing page, with a one-click download of the prototype.
  • Tested it from 360 to 1440 px wide, with the keyboard, for contrast, and offline.
Deliverables

What we delivered

  • A 10-slide stakeholder pitch.
  • A clickable prototype of both apps, about 40 screens, including sign-up with mobile verification, forgot password, an editable profile, and Terms and Privacy pages.
  • A public landing page with a one-click download of the prototype, live at barangayone.pages.dev.

After the panel review, we added an in-app footer with legal links, unified the type system to one font, and made the Terms and Conditions reachable from inside the app.
