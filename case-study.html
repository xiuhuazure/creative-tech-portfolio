難 The job

Two sides of one brand

Earth Moon Magick sells through Shopify and advertises on Meta and Instagram, so the work spans both the storefront and the creative that brings people to it. I handle both.

謀 What I do

Day to day

1. Blog and product pages, plus theme work

I build the brand’s blog pages and new product pages in GemPages, and make theme modifications with Shopify Liquid, HTML and CSS to improve the user experience and how layouts respond on different screens.

2. Product listings

I manage product listings end to end, troubleshooting variant configurations, media galleries and listing data directly in Shopify.

3. Meta video ads

I produce video ads from UGC and product footage, adding dynamic B-roll, motion text and audio design.

4. Instagram

As an Instagram assistant, I create feed and story assets, and publish and schedule content to keep the brand consistent.
