Two sides of one brand

謀 What I do

Day to day

I customize responsive landing pages using GemPages and make theme modifications with Shopify Liquid, HTML and CSS to improve the user experience and how layouts respond on different screens.

2. Product listings

I manage product listings end to end, troubleshooting variant configurations, media galleries and listing data directly in Shopify.

3. Meta video ads

I produce video ads from UGC and product footage, adding dynamic B-roll, motion text and audio design.

4. Ad graphics

I design on-brand static and motion graphics for Meta ad campaigns in Meta Business Suite, following mobile aspect ratios and the brand’s standard operating procedures.

5. Instagram

As an Instagram assistant, I create feed and story assets, draft copy, and publish and schedule content to keep the brand consistent.
